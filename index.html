How to Download Spotify Tracks?

You can download Spotify tracks using the online tool SpotiDost , a simple and powerful Spotify downloader tool. SpotiDost supports Spotify Tracks to MP3 conversions, Album Downloaders, and Playlist Downloaders. Follow the instructions below to download Spotify tracks on both mobile and PC editions.

For Mobile Users:

  1. Open the Spotify application on your mobile device (Android/iOS).
  2. Choose the track(s) that you want to download using SpotiDost Tracks Downloader .
  3. Tap the Share icon or ⋯ on the track.
  4. Select Copy Link .
  5. Open the SpotiDost Tracks Downloader .
  6. Paste the copied URL into the form and click the Download button.
  7. The verification process will begin - once completed, the download link will appear.
  8. Click the Download MP3 button to start the download.
  9. Wait 5–10 seconds for the downloading process to initiate.
  10. The song will be downloaded to your device at your chosen location.

FAQs

How do I download a playlist or album?

Paste the playlist/album link and press Download . We list all tracks; click Download MP3 next to each song.
